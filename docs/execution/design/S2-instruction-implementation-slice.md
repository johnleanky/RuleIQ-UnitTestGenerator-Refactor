## Reading Rules

Every Author IPM row appears exactly once below. `AUTHOR_KEEP` retains or moves the complete semantic responsibility, `AUTHOR_REPLACE` preserves the semantic responsibility while replacing legacy transport, `SPLIT` states the exact Scenario Author half and the separately owned downstream half, and `REMOVE_FROM_AUTHOR` prohibits that responsibility in effective Scenario Author instructions while retaining its future owner. `Prompt status` describes product implementation, not this design document.

A row may enter `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_VERIFIED` only after its destination text exists in `Main_Agent_Prompt.txt`; it becomes `VERIFIED` only after the required independent prompt-batch review. Before prompt work, every row remains `NOT_STARTED`.

## Completeness and Implementation Gates

- IDs must be the contiguous set `IPM-AUTH-001` through `IPM-AUTH-106`, with no duplicate.
- Owner counts must remain exactly 48 `SCENARIO_AUTHOR`, 39 `SHARED_CONTRACT`, 18 `UNIT_TEST_GENERATOR`, and one `VALIDATOR`.
- Action counts must remain exactly 48 Author keep/replace rows, 39 split rows, and 19 remove-from-Author rows.
- Each `SPLIT` row must have non-empty and non-overlapping Author and downstream responsibilities.
- Every `REMOVE_FROM_AUTHOR` row must remain absent from effective Scenario Author behavior and retain a named downstream destination.
- Prompt implementation must cite these IDs at bounded section comments or in an adjacent audit mapping so reverse traceability is mechanical.
- The ScenarioGroup grammar and fixtures govern all rows that materialize semantic handoff data; the prompt cannot introduce an incompatible private ledger.
- `Main_Agent.txt` remains reference-only and byte-identical to HEAD.

## Design-freeze Validation

A deterministic checker must parse this table and fail on a missing/duplicate ID, owner/action count drift, empty split half, missing downstream owner, invalid status, or unresolved evidence reference. Independent review must compare representative and high-risk rows to their exact source ranges, then reproduce the complete mechanical checks before product edits.
